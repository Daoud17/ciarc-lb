EDUCATION & TRAINING

Advancing Knowledge in Cardiovascular Imaging

CIARC Lebanon is committed to supporting education, professional development, and research in cardiovascular imaging. Our educational interests include clinical training, advanced imaging techniques, academic research, and the development of future specialists in cardiovascular medicine.

Clinical observerships provide opportunities to gain exposure to cardiovascular imaging workflows, image interpretation, and multidisciplinary clinical practice.

Areas of educational interest may include:

  • Cardiac CT examination workflows
  • Coronary CT angiography interpretation
  • Cardiovascular anatomy and pathology
  • Imaging post-processing techniques
  • Clinical case discussions
  • Research and academic activities

Program duration, eligibility, and supervision arrangements depend on the availability and structure of individual training opportunities.

Educational activities focused on developing theoretical and practical knowledge relevant to cardiac CT imaging and professional certification pathways.

  • Cardiac CT principles and protocols
  • Coronary anatomy and CT angiography
  • Radiation dose and image quality
  • Coronary artery calcium scoring
  • CAD-RADS reporting principles
  • Clinical interpretation and case review

Training may help participants prepare for relevant professional examinations, including EACVI-related educational pathways. Certification is awarded independently by the relevant professional organization.

Research-oriented educational opportunities may involve postgraduate students and researchers interested in cardiovascular imaging and quantitative analysis.

  • Cardiovascular imaging research methods
  • Coronary plaque characterization
  • Advanced CT reconstruction
  • Quantitative imaging biomarkers
  • Clinical research methodology
  • Scientific writing and publication

Formal postgraduate or doctoral supervision requires an appropriate academic partnership and institutional approval.

Medical students interested in cardiology, radiology, and cardiovascular imaging may explore opportunities to develop research skills through supervised projects.

  • Literature reviews and research planning
  • Cardiovascular imaging fundamentals
  • Clinical data analysis
  • Research presentations
  • Scientific poster preparation
  • Introduction to academic publishing

Project topics and participation depend on available research activities and supervision.

Focused educational workshops can provide structured learning around contemporary cardiovascular imaging techniques and clinical applications.

  • Cardiac CT image acquisition
  • 3D reconstruction and visualization
  • Coronary plaque analysis
  • CT-based procedural planning
  • Cardiac function and perfusion
  • Imaging quality assessment

Workshop formats may include lectures, imaging demonstrations, and case-based discussions.

Educational and research activities focused on the evaluation of artificial intelligence and quantitative imaging methods in cardiovascular medicine.

  • AI-assisted medical image analysis
  • Automated coronary plaque quantification
  • Image segmentation techniques
  • Clinical validation of imaging software
  • Accuracy and reproducibility assessment
  • Research data interpretation

The emphasis is on understanding and evaluating imaging technologies and their potential clinical applications.

Interested in Educational Opportunities?

To inquire about educational activities, research participation, or potential training opportunities, please contact CIARC Lebanon with a brief introduction, your CV, and your areas of interest.

Participation, duration, and any applicable conditions are subject to availability and institutional arrangements.
