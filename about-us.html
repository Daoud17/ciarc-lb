OUR APPROACH TO AI

Independent Clinical Validation

Artificial intelligence is becoming increasingly relevant to cardiovascular imaging, offering opportunities for automated measurements, image processing, and quantitative analysis. However, the clinical usefulness of AI-assisted imaging depends on the accuracy, reliability, and reproducibility of its results.

Independent clinical validation is essential for evaluating AI-based analysis tools against established reference standards. This includes assessing measurement consistency, identifying potential limitations, and examining how automated findings may contribute to clinical interpretation.

At CIARC Lebanon, we recognize the importance of evidence-based evaluation and responsible integration of emerging technologies into cardiovascular imaging and research.

Accuracy

Comparing imaging measurements with appropriate reference standards.

Reproducibility

Evaluating the consistency of analytical results across relevant conditions.

Clinical Relevance

Understanding how imaging analysis can support meaningful clinical assessment and decision-making.

Areas of Cardiovascular Imaging Validation
